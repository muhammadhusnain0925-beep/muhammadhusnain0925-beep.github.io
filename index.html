Inside AquaFlow

Keep daily work in one place.

The application saves business records in a local database on your Windows computer. Optional Google Drive backup helps you keep a separate copy.

01 / PRODUCTS

Products & categories

Organize your catalog with product pictures, categories, purchase and selling prices, and automatically assigned product identifiers.

02 / INVENTORY

Branch stock

Add stock, record sales and transfer products between branches while maintaining movement history and branch balances.

03 / SALES

Sales & reports

Select multiple products, review quantities and prices, and view sales performance and stock reports.

04 / DOCUMENTS

Invoices & estimates

Prepare documents with category-based product selection, pictures, quantities and prices. Print documents or save them as PDFs.

05 / LOCAL BACKUP

Database snapshots

Create a complete local backup of saved business records, including product pictures, and restore a compatible backup when needed.

06 / GOOGLE DRIVE

Your account, your backups

Connect your Google account to upload and download AquaFlow backups. Optional cleanup manages older backups created by the current installation.

Optional Google Drive connection

Save locally.
Keep a copy in Drive.

You choose whether to connect Google Drive. AquaFlow requests the limited drive.file permission to manage files created by or authorized for the application.

How Google data is used →
  1. Connect your Google accountUse Connect Google Drive in Settings and review Google's permission screen.
  2. Create a backupBackup Now saves and verifies a local database snapshot, then attempts its Drive upload.
  3. Recover when neededDownload a compatible backup and use Restore in AquaFlow. Review the selected file before replacing current records.
