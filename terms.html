These terms describe the use of AquaFlow Manager and its optional Google Drive backup integration. Any separately agreed written software licence, implementation or support agreement also applies to your installation.

1. Application purpose

AquaFlow Manager is Windows desktop software for managing product catalogs, branch inventory, sales, invoices, estimates and reports. It stores business records locally and can create database backups. This website provides product information and data policies.

2. Authorized use

Use the application with business information and accounts you are authorized to manage. You are responsible for the accuracy of records, prices, quantities, customer details and documents you enter or generate, and for your own business and reporting requirements.

3. Optional Google Drive connection

Connecting Google Drive requires your permission through Google's authorization screen. Backup files are stored in the account you select. Google account policies, storage availability, internet access and API availability can affect login, uploads and downloads.

Google services are subject to Google's own terms and pricing. AquaFlow does not promise unlimited storage or that Google's policies and free quotas will remain unchanged.

4. Backups and restoring records

Use Backup Now when you want to capture saved records. A local snapshot is created first; a failed cloud upload can remain pending. Review the application's status to confirm whether a cloud upload completed.

Keep an appropriate backup outside the computer that holds your working database. If you enable older-cloud-backup cleanup, eligible older copies are permanently removed according to the retention policy explained in the Privacy Policy.

Restore replaces the current database with the selected compatible backup. Review the selected backup and its date before restoring. A backup contains the saved records from its snapshot, so later work may need to be recovered separately.

5. Access and security

Protect your Windows account, local database files, backup copies and Google account. Business database backups are not automatically password-encrypted. You can disconnect the integration or revoke its Google permission at any time.

6. Availability and support

The application, operating system and third-party services can encounter interruptions or errors. Support, updates, installation assistance and custom work are provided according to the arrangements agreed with DevAxis. Contact the development team if a feature does not work as expected.

7. Privacy

The AquaFlow Manager Privacy Policy describes business records, Google data access, local token storage, backup retention and deletion controls.
